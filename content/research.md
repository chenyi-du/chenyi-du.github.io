# Research

## Intercity cooperation and post-disaster recovery
**Australian National University | 2023–present**

Exploring how intercity aid networks and urban connections shape resilience and post-disaster recovery. Methods include large-scale data integration, language models, network analysis, causal inference and machine learning.

## Environmental adaptation and individual behaviour

Studying why households adopt protective behaviours in response to environmental pollution and how those behaviours affect individual health and inequalities.

## Climate and sustainability research

Past projects include climate adaptation in agriculture, electric vehicle rebound effects, corporate green development, climate technologies and urban energy transitions.

## Computational social science

Experience analysing large-scale policy documents with language models, environmental and socioeconomic datasets, surveys, and panel data.
