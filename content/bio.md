I am a PhD researcher at the **Fenner School of Environment & Society, Australian National University**. My work lies at the intersection of climate change, urban resilience, environmental behaviour, and data-driven social science.

My current research examines how cities cooperate and exchange aid following disasters, how their networks shape recovery, and how cooperation might strengthen resilience. I combine large-scale text analysis, machine learning, network science, causal inference, satellite observations, and socioeconomic data.

Earlier work explored how individuals and households respond to air pollution, climate risks, and energy policies. I am interested in connecting rigorous quantitative evidence with policies that support sustainable and resilient societies.
